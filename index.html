Real-Time Multi-Modal Sensor for Field-Based Microplastic Detection

A buoy-style field sensor fusing three modalities in one laminar-flow microfluidic cell: multispectral optics (400–940 nm LED array), impedance spectroscopy (1 kHz–2 MHz), and 500× microscopy on an ESP32-S3. A YOLOv8-C (CSPDarknet53) segmentation model feeds an attention-weighted fusion network predicting polymer class (PET, PP, PE, PS, PVC), size, and a contamination score — 8-bit TFLite at under 200 ms per sample, benchmarked against FTIR/Raman, with a companion app for geotagged contamination heatmaps and a Microplastic Risk Index.
